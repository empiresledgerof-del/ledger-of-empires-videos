# Content plan: first 8 long videos (packaging first)

Rules applied (see strategy.md, "Lessons from top creators"):
- Title + thumbnail decided BEFORE the script; the first 5 s show what the thumbnail shows.
- Only two pillars for videos 1-8: **Bubbles & crashes** and **Great fortunes**, alternating.
- Two repeatable series: **"The Rise and Fall of ..."** (bubbles/crashes/dynasties) and **"How ... Got Rich"** (fortunes).
- Each long video gets 1 Short cut from its best moment (Related video -> the long video).
- Every fact and number is verified against 2+ reliable sources at script time; anything unconfirmed is cut.
- Title options are drafts; the run picks the strongest and may write better ones.
